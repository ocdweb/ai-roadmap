Anyone who will be hands-on with implementation — your operations manager, a key admin, or your most tech-savvy team member. Two people in the walk-through means faster adoption and someone to keep momentum after the session.

No. The roadmap is written for business owners, not engineers. Every automation recommendation comes with plain-English guidance, and we walk you through it live in the session. If you can use email, you can get started.

Yes. Smaller operators usually save the most time because they're doing every role themselves. The strategy is scaled to your team size — even if it's just you.
Will the recommended tools cost a lot every month? +
Most automations run on tools you are likely already paying for, or low-cost AI tools ($20–$50/month). We give you exact monthly costs as part of your strategy — no surprises.
